Projects

Feature-level image synthesis for industrial anomaly detection

Industry project with SEMES · IRIS Lab, Sungkyunkwan University

Semiconductor inspection has almost no defect images to train on. I built a framework that synthesizes them at the feature level — fusing continuous memory representations of normal and anomalous features, then guiding defect placement with a detector's reconstruction residuals — and studied training under severe class imbalance. Evaluated on MVTec AD.

Audio signal processing and spoofed-speech detection

Industry–academia project with Samsung Research · IRIS Lab, Sungkyunkwan University

Studied direction-of-arrival estimation for multi-channel audio, and trained AASIST-based detectors of synthetic speech on ASVspoof 5 and ASVspoof 2019, measuring how far they generalize to deepfake and TTS systems never seen in training.
